Scene 1 — The Encrypted Text
Right after Tarık's arrest, his phone buzzes with a message:
"Did you secure the locker room hard drive? Don't forget to cover your tracks."

You realize the ring was just a distraction! Someone is trying to steal the team's secret playbook data from the main server. It's a race against time to find out who accessed the server room.
💻 Setting: Sinan Erdem Arena's basement server room. It's dark, noisy with cooling fans, and highly restricted.
Quick Vocabulary Check
The mysterious text says "cover your tracks". Which sentence uses this idiom correctly?
💡 Hint: "Cover your tracks" means to hide evidence of what you've done, so nobody can follow or find you.
🕵️‍♂️ Idiom — "cover your tracks": To destroy or hide evidence of where you have been or what you have done. Derived from literal footprints in the dirt or snow!
⏱ Idiom — "a race against time": A situation where you have to do something very quickly before it's too late.
Scene 2 — Sequencing the Timeline
The system administrator confirms the hard drive was disconnected at exactly 9:15 PM. You look at the electronic door logs for the server room.

To solve this, you need to use Timeline Logic. We must find who was in the room at exactly 9:15 PM.

Timeline Logic Rules
BEFORE If they left before 9:15 PM → cleared.
AFTER If they arrived after 9:15 PM → cleared.
DURING If present at exactly 9:15 PM → prime suspect!
Logic Challenge 1 of 3
Look at Barış's log. He left the room at 9:10 PM. The theft happened at 9:15 PM. Based on timeline logic, what is his status?
💡 Hint: Is 9:10 PM before or after 9:15 PM? If he wasn't in the room when the theft happened, can he be guilty?
Scene 3 — Behind the Scenes
Barış is cleared. We are still in the dark about who actually pulled the drive. Let's look at Kerem, the rival team's scout. He claims he was lost in the basement.

The logs show Kerem swiped his badge at the server room door at 9:20 PM.
True or False — Quick Fire Round
Statement 1: Kerem had the opportunity to steal the drive at 9:15 PM.
💡 Hint: Opportunity means being in the right place at the right time. The drive was stolen at 9:15. Kerem arrived at 9:20. Did he have opportunity?
Statement 2: Therefore, Kerem is cleared from our suspect list for the actual theft.
💡 Hint: If someone did not have the opportunity to commit the crime because they arrived AFTER it happened, what does our Timeline Logic rule say?
🔦 Idiom — "in the dark": To not know about something that other people know about; to be lacking information.
Scene 4 — Grammar Timeout: Interrupted Actions
You bring in Zeynep, the Arena Manager. Her log shows she was inside from 9:00 PM to 9:30 PM.

Detectives need to know exactly what was happening when a crime occurred. To talk about a long background action interrupted by a short sudden action, we use the Past Continuous and the Past Simple.
Interruption Grammar Rules
Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing) = The long, background action.
Past Simple (V2) = The short action that interrupted it!
Example: "I was drinking coffee when the alarm rang."
Grammar Challenge
Zeynep explains her alibi. Fill in the blanks with the correct tenses:

"I _____________ (check) the cooling cables when the main screen suddenly _____________ (go) black."
💡 Hint: Checking the cables takes a long time (Past Continuous). The screen going black happens in one second (Past Simple).
Scene 5 — Caught Red-Handed
Zeynep claims the screen went black at exactly 9:15 PM. But you notice a USB security key sitting on her desk. You plug it into your laptop... and find the encrypted hard drive data transferring right now!

She's been caught red-handed. Let's make the final deduction based on the timeline.
Logic Challenge 3 of 3 — Final Deduction
Based on the Timeline Logic, why is Zeynep the ONLY person who could have stolen the drive at 9:15 PM?
💡 Hint: Review the logs. Barış left at 9:10. Kerem arrived at 9:20. When was Zeynep there?
🚨 Idiom — "caught red-handed": To be caught in the act of doing something wrong (originally referring to having blood on one's hands).
Scene 6 — The Empty Drive
"Alright, alright," Zeynep sighs. "I did it. Someone offered me a million Lira for the team's playbook data."

She opens her bag and hands you the physical hard drive. But when you plug it in... it's completely empty!

"I was copying the files when you walked in," she stammers. "The files aren't on this drive... they were transmitted to the VIP boxes upstairs!"
