The story says the mastermind "calls the shots." What does this mean?

👑 Idiom — "call the shots": To be the person in charge who makes the final decisions.
🏈 Idiom — "drop the ball": To make a mistake, especially by failing to do your responsibility.

Scene 4 — Grammar Timeout: Alternate Realities
Before you make the arrest, you think about how this all started. What if Zeynep (from Episode 2) hadn't made a mistake?

Detectives often analyze the past by imagining an alternate reality. To talk about an impossible situation in the past (because it already happened differently), we use the Third Conditional.
Third Conditional Rules
IF Clause If + Subject + had + Past Participle (V3)
Result Clause Subject + would have + Past Participle (V3)
Example: "If I had studied, I would have passed." (But I didn't study, so I failed).
Grammar Challenge
Zeynep copied the files, so the boss got them. Which sentence uses the Third Conditional correctly to describe the OPPOSITE of what actually happened?
💡 Hint: Look for "had + V3" in the IF part, and "would have + V3" in the result part.
