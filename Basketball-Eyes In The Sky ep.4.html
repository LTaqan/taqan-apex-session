Quick Vocabulary Check

Scene 3 — Grammar Timeout: The Passive Voice
You find the location! You need to report this to Coach Demir, but police and detectives often speak in a very specific way. Instead of saying "The radar tracked the helicopter," they focus on the *object* of the action. This is called the Passive Voice.
Passive Voice Rules
Rule Use Verb 'to be' + Past Participle (V3).
Active The radar (subject) tracked the helicopter (object).
Passive The helicopter (object) was tracked by the radar.
Grammar Challenge
Coach Demir asks: "Are they downloading the playbook data right now?" How do you reply correctly using the Present Continuous Passive?
💡 Hint: The action is happening *right now*. We need "is being" + V3.
