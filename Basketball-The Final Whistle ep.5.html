Scene 2 — The Password Protocol
Cenk laughs. "Arrest me if you want! It's down to the wire. In 60 seconds, the upload will finish and your team's strategies will be mine."

He smashes the keyboard, locking the computer. The screen demands a 3-digit cancellation code to stop the upload.

You look closely at his desk and find a sticky note with three mathematical rules.
Decryption Logic Challenge
Password Rules (3 Digits)
Rule 1 All three digits are different numbers.
Rule 2 The first digit is exactly half of the second digit.
Rule 3 If you add all three digits together, the sum is 12.
The screen flashes: UPLOAD: 98%. What is the only possible 3-digit code?
💡 Hint: Let's test the options.
Is the first digit half of the second? (4 is half of 8. 2 is half of 4).
Does the total equal 12? (4+8+0 = 12. 2+4+6 = 12).
Wait! Read Rule 2 again closely... Wait, let's look at the options. Only ONE option fits ALL rules!
⏳ Idiom — "down to the wire": A situation whose outcome is not decided until the very last second!
