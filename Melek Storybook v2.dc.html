Chapter I · A2

The Quiet Canvas

Melek sits at the back of the art room, brush in hand. She mixes purple and green until the color feels right.

She does not raise her hand in English class. Words feel slower than paint. But her sketchbook is full: courts, nets, a girl mid-jump. She has not shown anyone yet.

First practice, first serve
Chapter II · A2 to B1

First Serve

Melek joins the volleyball team because her cousin asked twice. She learns the words for the game before she trusts her own accent: serve, rotate, out of bounds.

Her first serve hits the net. She says "sorry" in English without thinking. Nobody laughs. She tries again.

volleyball practice nerves
Melek calls the play
Chapter III · B1 to B2

Finding Her Voice

By February, Melek calls the plays. Melissa passes, Melek sets, someone else spikes. Between points they talk about weekend plans and a painting Melek is finishing.

Her sentences get longer. She corrects herself less. She corrects Melissa's Turkish instead.

teamwork confidence friendship
Captains for the day
Chapter IV · B2

Center Court

Melek stands at center court, Melissa beside her, both team captains for the day. She argues a bad call in full sentences.

After the match she shows her sketchbook to the whole team: a court and a canvas, painted as one picture. Nobody is surprised anymore.
