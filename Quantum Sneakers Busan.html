Scene 1 — The Alley in Busan
It was a normal Saturday afternoon in Busan, South Korea. Yoonu was walking through the crowded, narrow alleys of Gukje Market.

He walked into a dusty, old antique shop. In the corner, underneath a pile of old books, Yoonu saw something incredible. It was a shoebox, but it was glowing with a bright cyan light!
Vocabulary Check
The story says Yoonu walked into an "antique" shop. What does antique mean?
💡 Hint: Gukje Market has many shops selling things from 50 or 100 years ago.
Scene 2 — Grammar Timeout
Yoonu opened the box. Inside were the coolest sneakers he had ever seen. They were black with glowing blue lines.

Because this happened in the past, we use the Past Simple Tense. But beware! Some verbs do not end in "-ed". They are irregular!
Irregular Past Verbs
See Changes to: saw
Find Changes to: found
Buy Changes to: bought
Grammar Challenge
Fill in the blank correctly:

"Yoonu ________ the glowing sneakers and immediately ________ them."
💡 Hint: What is the past tense of 'find' and 'buy'? Do we say 'finded'?
