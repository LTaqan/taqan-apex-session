Level 1 — A Tiny Problem
You stepped through the portal into Dimension X. But when you look around, everything is gigantic!

A blade of grass looks like a skyscraper. A normal ant looks like a scary monster. The portal didn't transport you to a new world... it shrunk you!

You hear the Professor's voice through your earpiece. "Sean! You are now microscopic! You are inside a drop of water!"
Vocabulary Check
The Professor says you are "microscopic". What does this mean?
💡 Hint: Think about what a 'microscope' is used for!
Level 2 — Grammar Timeout
As you walk around, you see giant green plant cells floating in the water.

You want to tell the Professor about your life experiences. We use the Present Perfect tense to talk about experiences we have had (or haven't had) in our life!
Present Perfect Rules
Form Have / Has + Past Participle (V3)
Keywords We often use "ever" (in questions) and "never" (in negatives).
Example: "Have you ever seen a giant plant cell?"
Grammar Challenge
Fill in the blank correctly:

"I ________ never ________ an insect this big in my whole life!"
💡 Hint: Look at the rule! We need "Have" + the V3 past participle of see.
Level 3 — The Un-Shrink Ray
The robot dog runs past you, dropping the notebook! You grab it.

Inside, you find the instructions to build an Un-Shrink Ray to get back to normal size. But the instructions are scrambled!
Sequencing Challenge
Place the steps in the logical order to build the ray and escape:
Connect the battery to the laser.
Grow back to normal size.
Find the broken laser piece.
Point the laser at yourself and fire!

Level 4 — The Final Charge
The laser is built! But it needs your voice to charge up. You must speak the truth about your experiences to power it.
Grammar Check
Which sentence is grammatically correct to power the laser?
💡 Hint: Remember the rule: "Have" + Past Participle (V3). The V3 of 'be' is 'been'.
