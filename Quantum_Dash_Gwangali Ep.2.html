Scene 1 — The Black Van
Yoonu ran away from the market and arrived at Gwangalli Beach. The beautiful bridge was glowing over the water.

Suddenly, he looked back. The mysterious black van was following him! Yoonu realized he needed to activate the full power of the sneakers to escape across the beach.
Vocabulary Check
What does it mean to "activate" the sneakers?
💡 Hint: Think about what you do when you want your phone or computer to start working!
Scene 2 — Grammar Timeout
Yoonu looked at the shoes. He realized these sneakers gave him superhuman abilities!

We use Modals of Ability to talk about things we have the power to do.
Ability Rules
Past Ability Use Could or Couldn't (Yesterday, I couldn't run fast.)
Present Ability Use Can or Can't (Today, I can run super fast!)
Grammar Challenge
Fill in the blank correctly:

"Before he found the shoes, Yoonu ________ jump very high. But now, he ________ jump over a car!"
💡 Hint: First blank is in the PAST (negative). Second blank is in the PRESENT (positive).
Scene 3 — The Startup Sequence
The black van stops, and men in suits step out! Yoonu remembers the instructions he saw glowing on the inside of the shoebox.

He must follow the exact steps to activate Quantum Speed!
Sequencing Challenge
Place the steps in the logical order to run away:
Tap your heels together twice.
Run across the beach at the speed of sound!
Tie the laces tightly.
Say the password: "Quantum Dash!"

Scene 4 — Over the Water
Yoonu takes off! He is running so fast that when he hits the edge of Gwangalli Beach, he doesn't sink. He is running directly *over* the ocean waves!
Grammar Check
Which sentence correctly describes Yoonu right now?
💡 Hint: This is happening right NOW. So we need the present modal of ability!
