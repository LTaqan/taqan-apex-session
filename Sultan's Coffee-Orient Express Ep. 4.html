Scene 1 — All Aboard!
Agent Yusuf, Bob's latest vacation took him to the year 1883. He boarded the inaugural journey of the legendary Orient Express, traveling from Istanbul to Paris.

It is the most luxurious train in the world. The problem? Bob brought a futuristic digital camera with him. He was taking photos of his dinner, but when he went to the bathroom, someone stole it!

If that camera reaches Paris, the French will reverse-engineer it and jump to the 21st Century overnight! You must find the thief.
Idiom Check
You tell Bob, "We need to find the camera to keep history on track." What does this idiom mean?
💡 Hint: Think of a train. If it stays on the track, it goes where it's supposed to go.
✨ Vocabulary: Luxurious — Extremely comfortable, elegant, and expensive.
🚂 Idiom: On track — Proceeding as planned or expected.
Scene 2 — Grammar Timeout: Unfinished Business
You begin interrogating the passengers. You ask the French Chef a question, but you need to be careful with your tenses!

Detectives use the Past Simple for finished time periods (yesterday, last week). But they use the Present Perfect for unfinished time periods (today, this week) or life experiences.
Present Perfect vs. Past Simple
Past Simple "Did you see the camera yesterday?" (Yesterday is finished).
Present Perfect "Have you seen the camera today?" (Today is not finished yet).
Grammar Challenge
Which question is grammatically correct to ask the Chef about an event happening this morning (which is not over yet)?
💡 Hint: "This morning" is unfinished time, so we need "Have you...".
Scene 3 — The Passenger Manifest
The Chef is terrified. "I didn't take it! But I'm going to throw someone under the bus. I saw someone running into the First Class cabins."

You have three suspects in First Class:
Cabin A: The British Banker
Cabin B: The Russian Countess
Cabin C: The French Chef (His private room)
Detective Logic Rules
Rule 1 The thief is NOT in Cabin A.
Rule 2 The Countess is next door to the Chef's private room.
Rule 3 The thief is NOT the Chef.
Logic Challenge: Cabin Hunt
Based on the rules, which cabin contains the thief?
💡 Hint: If the thief is not in A, and not the Chef (C), who is left?
🕵️ Vocabulary: Suspect (noun) — A person thought to be guilty of a crime.
🚌 Idiom: Throw under the bus — To betray a friend or colleague for selfish reasons. (Funny to use on a train!)
Scene 4 — The Flash
You burst into Cabin B! The Russian Countess screams. She is holding Bob's digital camera upside down. She presses the button, and the flash goes off, temporarily blinding her!

"What is this witchcraft?!" she yells.

You grab the camera. "It's just a magic box, Countess. I'll take that." You and Bob run to the back of the train. It's time to escape before the train reaches Paris!
True or False — Idiom Check
Statement: If you tell Bob to run "full steam ahead", you are telling him to move as fast as possible with all his energy.
💡 Hint: "Full steam ahead" comes from the days of steam trains, meaning moving at maximum speed!
