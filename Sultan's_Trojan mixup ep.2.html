Scene 1 — A Massive Mistake
Agent Yusuf, we have another crisis on our hands.

Bob took a vacation to the year 1200 BC to watch the Trojan War. The Greeks built a gargantuan wooden horse, hid soldiers inside, and left it as a "gift" for the city of Troy.

Bob snuck into the horse for a selfie. He dropped his modern, talking GPS navigation device inside it! The Trojans are about to pull the horse into the city. If they hear a robotic voice saying "Turn left," the great deception will be ruined!
Idiom Check
The Trojans accepted the wooden horse without checking inside. We have an idiom in English: "Never look a gift horse in the mouth." What does it mean?
💡 Hint: If someone gives you a free gift, should you complain about it or look for flaws?
📚 Vocabulary: Gargantuan — Extremely huge; gigantic.
🎭 Vocabulary: Deception — The act of hiding the truth or tricking someone.
Scene 2 — Inside the Gates
You step out of your portal as the sun sets. The Trojans have pulled the horse inside their high walls. The city is celebrating.

You need to climb up the leg of the horse, but there are thousands of people around. Finding the right moment to climb is like finding a needle in a haystack.

You put on a cloak and hide near the horse, listening to two Trojan guards talking.
True or False
Statement 1: If finding a moment is "like finding a needle in a haystack," it means it is a very easy task.
🌾 Idiom: A needle in a haystack — Something that is almost impossible to find.
Scene 3 — Grammar Timeout
The guards are arguing. One of them says, "This is strange. I ______________ (never / see) a wooden horse this big before!"

When we talk about life experiences from the past leading up to the *present moment*, we use the Present Perfect!
Present Perfect (Experience)
Form Subject + have/has + Past Participle (V3)
Example: "Have you ever eaten Turkish delight?"

Scene 4 — Inside the Belly
The guards walk away. You quickly climb up a rope and slip inside a hidden trapdoor.

It is pitch black inside. There are 30 Greek soldiers hiding in here.

If you make a loud noise, you will wake the soldiers, and they will let the cat out of the bag too early! You hear the robotic voice of the GPS echoing from deeper inside.
True or False
Statement: If the Greeks "let the cat out of the bag", it means they will accidentally reveal their secret surprise attack too early.
🐈 Idiom: Let the cat out of the bag — To accidentally reveal a secret.
Scene 5 — The GPS Puzzle
The horse has three internal storage compartments. You have to find the correct compartment quickly. The robotic GPS voice says: "Recalculating route..."
Compartment Clues
1 The Head (Very top, near the horse's ears).
2 The Upper Belly (Where the Greek soldiers are sleeping).
3 The Lower Hooves (Storage for Greek swords).

Bob's Log: "I took a selfie near the ears, but then I climbed down below the sleeping soldiers to look at their shiny swords. I dropped my phone down there!"
Logic Challenge
Based on Bob's log, which compartment contains the GPS?
Scene 6 — The Great Escape
You climb down to the Lower Hooves. You find the GPS device glowing between two bronze swords!

Just as you pick it up, the GPS voice yells: "YOU HAVE ARRIVED AT YOUR DESTINATION!"

A Greek soldier wakes up. You dive out of the hidden trapdoor in the horse's leg, landing safely outside. You open your time portal and escape by the skin of your teeth!
