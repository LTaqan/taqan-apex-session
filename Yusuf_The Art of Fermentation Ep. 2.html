in a pickle
💡 "In a pickle" means in a difficult or awkward situation. Yes, it comes from actual pickling — being preserved in brine means being stuck!
a slow burn
💡 "A slow burn" describes something that develops gradually but intensifies over time — exactly like fermentation. Also used for emotions and stories.
ferment trouble
💡 "To ferment trouble" means to quietly cause problems or unrest — the trouble 'grows' like a fermentation culture, unseen until it's too late.
raw deal
💡 "A raw deal" means being treated unfairly. 'Raw' here means unprocessed, incomplete — you didn't get the full, cooked version of fairness.
bring to the boil
💡 "Bring something to the boil" means to reach a critical, intense point — emotions, situations, or conflicts that have been simmering for a while.
past its sell-by date
💡 "Past its sell-by date" means outdated, no longer useful or relevant — borrowed directly from food labelling language.
