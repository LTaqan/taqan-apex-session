Comprehension questions

  1. Where do the three little cats live?
  2. Why does Mırmır build a wooden house?

  4. Why does the brick house survive?
  5. What does the dog promise?

Full clean text

  1. Once upon a time, three little cats lived with their mother near a forest in Sakarya.
  2. One day, Mother Cat said, “It is time to build your own houses.”
  3. Mırmır said, “I will build a house of wood. It will be fast!”
  4. Pofuduk said, “I will build a house of cat food. It will be delicious!”
  5. Captain Meow said, “I will build a house of bricks. It will be strong.”
  6. Then a big hungry dog came out of the forest.
  7. The dog went to the wooden house. “Let me in!” he barked.
  8. Crash! The wooden house fell down, and Mırmır ran away.
  9. Mırmır ran to Pofuduk’s cat food house. The dog followed him.
  10. The dog sniffed the house. “Mmm... chicken!” he said.
  11. Crunch, crunch! The dog ate the cat food house.
  12. The two cats ran to Captain Meow’s brick house.
  13. The dog barked, jumped, and pushed. But the brick house did not move.
  14. Captain Meow smiled. “A strong house is the best house.”
  15. The dog sat down. He looked tired and hungry.
  16. The cats gave the dog some food. “Please do not eat cats or houses,” they said.
  17. The dog said, “I promise. Can I eat homework?”
  18. “Only math homework,” said Mırmır.
  19. From that day, they all lived happily in Sakarya.
  20. The end.
